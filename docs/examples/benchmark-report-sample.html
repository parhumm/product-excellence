1

Say what the product is on the first screen

High impactSeen in screenshotCopy: Alpha
TodayNorthwind · page source
<meta name="robots" content="noindex nofollow">

The first heading is “Popular now”, and the only main button goes straight to payment. The page also asks search engines not to index it.

Best examples
  • Alpha: “All your films and series in one place”, then “From €5.99 a month. Cancel anytime.”
  • Alpha puts its minimum term in small print right under the button.
Do this
  • Add a one-line headline above the posters that says what the service is and who it is for.
  • Show a “from” price right below it, and “cancel anytime” only if that is true.
  • Remove noindex from public pages.
2

State plainly whether plans renew automatically

High impactSeen in screenshotCopy: Alpha
Today

Nothing says whether a plan renews, when, at what price, or how to cancel, and the page has no footer.

Best examples
  • Alpha: “Renews automatically at the same price until you cancel.”
  • Alpha: “Cancel in Account. You keep access until the end of your billing period.”
Do this
  • First confirm with Payments whether plans renew.
  • Then add one sentence in body text under the plans, and link it to a page that explains cancelling.
  • Add Help, Terms, Refunds and Devices links under the plans.

Read before sharing

Limits of this benchmark

  • Gamma did not answer in run 22222222 (blocked). The page showed "Something went wrong"; no plans appeared.
  • Each page was visited once, so speed figures vary between visits.
  • Rankings are the review AI's judgement of what each page showed, not traffic or conversion data.
  • The tool masks password, email, phone and one-time-code fields in screenshots; a filled box over one is not part of the site.
  • Browsing was read-only: nothing was signed up for, bought or submitted.
Corrections

Where a run summary was wrong
