# Schema.org vocabulary

`schemaorg.jsonld` was downloaded from the official machine-readable vocabulary endpoint on 2026-09-05:

https://schema.org/version/latest/schemaorg-current-https.jsonld

Source and attribution: Schema.org, https://schema.org/docs/developers.html

The engine uses the vocabulary offline for known type/property and property-domain checks. It does not fetch page-supplied JSON-LD contexts. These checks do not certify rich-result eligibility or resolve every possible external JSON-LD context.
